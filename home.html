What it offers

Everything is built around one goal: filling a living Pokédex without juggling spreadsheets and wiki tabs.

Every game, one place

Pick a game and track it on its own. Switch between the National Dex and the game's regional Dex, and follow the normal list or the shiny list.

Where to find it

Tap the location button on any Pokémon to see where it appears, with the rates, levels and methods. Games that can send Pokémon to yours are included.

Maps with outlines

Region maps show where each Pokémon lives. Press "Show on map" to outline the exact route or area.

Seasons and time of day

Filter by season in Black and White, or by morning, day and night in Gold, Silver, Crystal, Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver. Rates update to match.

Evolution help

See exactly how a Pokémon evolves, and where to get the stone or item it needs, marked as one-time or repeatable.

The odd encounters, explained

Roaming Pokémon, honey trees, Shadow Pokémon, N's Pokémon, and extras like the PokéWalker and Dream Radar all come with a short plain-language explanation.

Shinies and forms

Track shinies separately, and see which forms exist and how to get them. In Settings you can choose whether gender differences and alternate forms count toward completion.

Trainer card and diploma

Fill in your trainer card, then save a pixel-style diploma as a PNG once the Pokédex is complete, in the style of the game you played.

Made for quick tapping

Search by name or number, filter to what is missing, and use Select mode to mark whole ranges at once. There is an Undo button too. PC box view groups Pokémon the way the game does.

Themes, light and dark

Choose from a whole set of themes inspired by the games, each with a light and a dark version.

Works offline

Add it to your home screen and keep tracking without a connection, whether you are on a train or at the back of a cave.

Free, with no account

There is nothing to sign up for. Open it and start catching.

How it works

You can be up and running in under a minute.

  1. Choose your gameOpen the tracker and pick the game you are playing from the Game menu.
  2. Tap to markTap a Pokémon when you catch it. Tap the location button when you need help finding one.
  3. Finish and celebrateReach 100% and unlock your diploma. Save it as a picture and share it.
